## LinkedIn post: fee tracker

One invoice. Two instalments. A balance someone still has to check.

If your coaching centre manages fees in a spreadsheet, a consistent record helps your team answer:

- What was billed?
- What payment has actually arrived?
- What needs attention next?

We've made a free fee-tracker CSV with fictional worked examples for partial payments, paid invoices and overpayment credits. The guide explains how to set it up, plus optional spreadsheet formulas you can add.

No account or email required. It's a manual template; it does not reconcile payments or send reminders automatically.

## LinkedIn post: teacher handover

When a teacher is away, the covering teacher needs more than a meeting link.

They need the correct time zone, the lesson objective, the right worksheet version and a clear place to leave the next teacher's notes.

Our free teacher-handover checklist covers preparation, the class itself and the follow-up. Download the editable text file and adapt it to your centre's process.

No signup required. Completing the checklist does not change your timetable or teacher assignments; your coordinator still confirms those changes.

## Publishing and review

- Start with the resource that best matches the audience of an existing channel. Share the second when it is relevant, rather than repeating both links in every post.
- Use a screenshot of the resource's example or download section as a visual. Keep fictional example records labelled.
- Answer questions about the actual template and collect suggestions for improvements.
- Measure visits and downloads alongside enquiries. Track the campaign's landing pages, not just likes or total sessions.
- Compare like-for-like periods after publication. There is no guaranteed traffic increase or fixed ranking timetable.
