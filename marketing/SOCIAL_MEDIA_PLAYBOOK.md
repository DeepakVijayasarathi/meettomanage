## 1. The business in one page

**What we sell:** Meet to Manage is coaching-centre and academy management software. One platform for:

- Interactive live classes (own classroom, not a meeting app), recordings (15-day window)
- Enquiries, demo classes and admissions CRM
- Batch scheduling, teacher availability, attendance
- Automated fee collection (Razorpay / Cashfree), invoices, receipts, discounts, promo codes
- Parent app and parent communication (email, SMS, WhatsApp notifications)
- Reports and dashboards for owners
- 8 portals/roles: Admin, Relationship Manager, Admission Team, Teacher, Parent, Student, Coordinator, Management

**Core message:** *Running a coaching centre should not mean juggling five different tools.*

**Main call to action:** Book a free platform walkthrough → https://meettomanage.cloud/get-started

## 3. Brand voice and writing rules

- Write like a helpful operator, not an advertiser. Practical, calm, specific.
- Lead with the owner's problem, not the product. Product comes last, briefly.
- Plain words, short lines, contractions. One idea per post.
- Use Indian context naturally (₹, parents, batches, WhatsApp, coaching centre).
- At most one question per post.
- Avoid: buzzwords, hype ("revolutionary", "game-changer"), long hashtag walls, fake urgency, invented stats.
- Be honest about limits (e.g. "It's a manual template; it does not send reminders automatically").
- Never claim a customer result, quote, ranking or traffic number that isn't real and approved.
- Label fictional examples as fictional.

## 6. Daily LinkedIn routine

### Posting rules

| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Frequency | 1 post per day on the company page; founder reshares or writes own version 3–5×/week |
| Best time (IST) | 8:30–9:30 AM on weekdays; 10:00 AM weekends |
| Length | 120–220 words for text posts |
| Link | Put the article link in the **first comment** (LinkedIn reduces reach for links in the post body). Mention "Link in the first comment." |
| Visual | Blog cover image from `public/blog/<slug>.webp`, a 5–8 slide PDF carousel, or a product screenshot |
| Engagement | Reply to every comment within 2 hours. Spend 15 minutes commenting on posts by academy owners |

### Weekly format rotation

| Day | Format | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Problem / insight post (text) | Name a pain owners feel this week |
| Tuesday | How-to carousel (PDF document post) | Teach a checklist from an article |
| Wednesday | Article share | Drive blog traffic |
| Thursday | Product in action (screenshot or 20–40s video) | Show how Meet to Manage solves it |
| Friday | Free resource / template | Lead magnet with no signup |
| Saturday | Story, case study or poll | Trust and conversation |
| Sunday | Light: weekly tip roundup or question | Keep the page active |

### 30-minute daily checklist

1. Open today's row in the calendar (§7).
2. Copy the matching template (§8), fill in the hook and 3 takeaways from the article.
3. Attach the visual. Build the UTM link (§18).
4. Publish at 8:30–9:30 AM IST. Add the link as the first comment.
5. Founder/team: like + thoughtful comment within the first hour.
6. Reply to comments; DM anyone who asks for a demo with the `/get-started` link.
7. Log the post in the tracking sheet (date, pillar, format, link, impressions, clicks, comments).

## 11. How to publish a blog post

1. Sign in at `/marketing/login` (Marketing role).
2. Go to **Blog Posts** (`/marketing/blog-posts`) → New post.
3. Fill title, slug, excerpt, content (headings use `## `), cover image, keyword.
4. Tick **Published**. To schedule, set **Publish at** to a future time — the post stays hidden until then (backend: `BlogService` only shows posts where `IsPublished && PublishedAtUtc <= now`).
5. Save. The post appears at `/blog/<slug>` and in the blog sitemap (`/api/blog/sitemap.xml`).
6. Add internal links to the pillar page and to a resource or solution page.
7. Add the slug to the LinkedIn calendar.

Notes:
- The live blog reads from the database. Content in `src/data/blogPosts.ts` only shows in demo mode.
- API: public `GET /api/blog`, `GET /api/blog/{slug}`; admin `GET/POST/PUT/DELETE /api/blog/admin`.
- Keep `public/sitemap.xml` in sync when adding new marketing routes.

Built into the product: **Marketing Console → Cold email** (`/marketing/outreach`), backend `MarketingOutreachController`, `OutreachService`.

### How it works

1. **Import prospects** — CSV upload (`Email, First name, Academy, City, Region, Phone, Source, Notes`) or add manually.
2. **AI lead qualification** scores each prospect.
3. **Campaigns** — multi-step email sequences (subject + body per step, delay days). Can be AI-drafted, previewed, and test-sent to yourself.
4. **Enrol** prospects into a campaign and set it active.
5. **Sending engine** (background, every minute) sends at most one due email at a time, spaced evenly across sending hours, respecting the daily limit.
6. **Replies** — Zoho IMAP monitor (`imappro.zoho.in:993`, every 60s) or webhook `POST /api/marketing/outreach/inbound/reply` with header `X-Outreach-Webhook-Secret`. A reply **stops all follow-ups** for that prospect, gets classified by AI, and creates a sales task if needed.
7. **Unsubscribe** link in every email (`/api/marketing/outreach/unsubscribe/{token}`). Replied, unsubscribed or bounced prospects are never emailed again.

### Settings (Cold email → Settings)

| Setting | Default / notes |
| --- | --- |
| From name / address, Reply-To | Dedicated outreach mailbox |
| Daily limit | 50 (max 500) |
| Sending window | Start/end hour, time zone `Asia/Kolkata` |
| Min follow-up days | Minimum gap between steps |
| Max failures per step | 3 |
| Footer / company line | Legal footer text |
| Switches | Email, campaign sending, manual email, reply sending, reply sync, AI (qualification, drafting, campaign generation, reply assistant, insights) |

### Recommended sequence (4 emails)

| Step | Day | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0 | Short, specific problem for their niche + one question. No attachments. |
| 2 | +2 | Proof: one real, approved customer result or case study link |
| 3 | +3 | Free offer: fee tracker / handover checklist, or a question about how they do X today |
| 4 | +5 | Short last note, door open |

Console screens also include: Today (daily queue), History (with retry), Replies (thread, AI analysis, send reply, mark handled), unmatched replies queue, sync history, Growth (reactivation, AI plan), Analytics and insights.
