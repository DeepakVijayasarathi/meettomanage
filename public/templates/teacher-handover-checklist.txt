BEFORE CLASS
[ ] Confirm the covering teacher has accepted the session and time zone.
[ ] Confirm staff access through the approved portal. Never share a login.
[ ] Share the lesson objective and where the previous class stopped.
[ ] Identify the worksheet / slides / activity, including version or page.
[ ] Share essential learner support notes through the approved staff channel.
[ ] Confirm who will tell families about any change and by when.
[ ] Check whether recording is available and appropriate for this session.
[ ] Name a coordinator to contact if access or classroom tools fail.

DURING CLASS
[ ] Open the correct session and verify the batch.
[ ] Check audio, video and the resource before learners join.
[ ] Follow the agreed joining / waiting-room process.
[ ] Check attendance and note any joining issues.
[ ] Record lesson progress and the practice task assigned.

AFTER CLASS
[ ] Confirm attendance records and flag discrepancies to the coordinator.
[ ] Record the topic and pages actually completed.
[ ] Save the practice task and resource version for the next teacher.
[ ] Share a concise update through the agreed parent communication process.
[ ] Record open questions, access issues and the owner of each follow-up.
[ ] Return the handover to the usual teacher / coordinator.
