**Listing Type:** Software Directory

**Company Name:** Meet to Manage
**Tagline:** Academy Management Software for Coaching Centres
**Short Description:** All-in-one platform for coaching institutes: live classes, admissions, fee collection, attendance and parent app. Built for Indian academies, tuition centres and online tutors.
**Detailed Description:** Meet to Manage is academy management software designed for coaching institutes, tuition centres, online academies and individual tutors in India. The platform brings everything together — live video classes with whiteboard and screen sharing, automatic fee collection with Razorpay and Cashfree integration, admissions CRM with demo booking pipeline, attendance tracking, a branded parent mobile app, homework and quizzes, and granular team permissions. Unlike tools that require separate integrations for video, payments and scheduling, Meet to Manage runs all core academy operations on one platform with simple per-centre pricing starting free. Centres can start with 25 students at zero cost, then scale to hundreds of students across multiple subjects and branches.

- Attendance tracking and class recordings
- Team roles with granular permissions
- Homework, quizzes and certificates
- WhatsApp notifications and bulk messaging
- Free plan supports up to 25 students
- Paid plans from ₹999/month
