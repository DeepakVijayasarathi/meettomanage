## The 2026 academy software landscape
**Running a coaching centre or online academy has never been simpler — until you try to stitch together Zoom, spreadsheets, WhatsApp and a payment gateway.** Every tool works, but nothing talks to each other.

That is why more academy owners in India are switching to all-in-one platforms. The question is no longer whether to consolidate, but which platform fits your centre best.

## What "best" actually means for your academy
Before comparing features, decide what matters most to you. A phonics teacher needs different tools than a multi-branch coaching centre. Here is what most owners care about:

• Live classroom quality — video, whiteboard, screen sharing, recordings
• Fee collection — automatic invoices, reminders, online payment, fee suspension
• Admissions — demo booking, enquiry tracking, follow-up sequences
• Parent app — schedule, attendance, recordings, payments, messages
• Team roles — admin, teacher, parent, admissions, management portals
• Pricing — simple per-centre pricing or per-student fees that grow with you

## How Meet to Manage compares to alternatives

### Classplus
Classplus is popular for coaching centres that want a branded app. It focuses heavily on content delivery and parent communication. However, it does not include a live classroom, admission CRM or automatic fee collection natively. If your primary need is content delivery rather than live teaching and administration, Classplus works. For centres that want live classes, fees and admissions in one place, Meet to Manage covers everything without separate integrations.

### Topshelf
Topshelf is widely used for attendance and fee tracking in Indian coaching centres. It handles attendance and fee management well but does not include live classes, a parent app for video content or an admissions CRM. Centres using Topshelf often still need Zoom for classes and a separate tool for parent communication. Meet to Manage replaces all three with one system.

### Class4all
Class4all targets K-12 schools with homework distribution and parent communication. It is not designed for coaching centres or adult education. If you run a coaching institute, tutoring centre or online academy, Meet to Manage is the better fit because it is built for centre operations rather than school curricula.

### Eduprise
Eduprise focuses on student assessment and exam preparation content. It is excellent for test prep centres but does not include live classroom software, automatic fee collection or admissions CRM. For centres that deliver live classes and need full operations management, Meet to Manage is the more complete platform.

### TutorCruncher
TutorCruncher is a UK-based platform popular for one-to-one tutoring businesses. It handles scheduling, invoicing and client management well but does not include a built-in live classroom, parent app or class recordings. Indian coaching centres that teach groups and need video classes should consider Meet to Manage instead.
