# Google Business Profile — Optimization Checklist

This is a manual task. No code changes needed. Follow these steps in order.

---

## Step 1: Create or Claim Your Profile

1. Go to https://business.google.com/
2. Click **Manage now**
3. Enter business name: **Meet to Manage**
4. If it exists → Click **Claim this business**
5. If it doesn't → Click **Add your business** and follow the setup wizard

---

## Step 2: Verify Your Business

Google will send a verification code. Choose one method:

### Postcard (recommended — free)
- Google mails a postcard to your business address with a 5-digit code
- Takes 5-14 days
- Enter the code in your Google Business Profile

### Phone verification
- Available if Google has your phone number on file
- You'll get a text or call with a code

### Email verification
- Rarely available — depends on Google's records

### Video verification (newer option)
- Record a short video showing your business premises, equipment and documents
- Google reviews within a few days

## Step 5: Get Reviews

### How to get more reviews
- [ ] Add a review request link to the parent app footer
- [ ] Add a review request email after trial completion
- [ ] Ask happy customers directly via WhatsApp
- [ ] Add a link in email signatures

### Review request link format
https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=YOUR_PLACE_ID

(Replace YOUR_PLACE_ID after Google assigns it)

### Sample review request message
> Hi [Name], thanks for using Meet to Manage! If you're happy with the platform, could you leave us a quick review on Google? It helps us reach more academy owners like you. https://[review-link]

## Step 6: Post Regularly

Google Business Posts are like mini-blog posts that show in your profile. Post at least once a month.

### Post ideas:
- New feature launch (e.g., "We just added automated fee reminders!")
- Case study (e.g., "How The Reader Nest scaled to 200 students")
- Blog post share (link to any blog post on mettomanage.cloud/blog)
- Offer (e.g., "Start your free plan today — no credit card required")
- Event (webinar, product update)

### Post format:
- [ ] Post type: Update
- [ ] Photo: Branded image or screenshot
- [ ] Text: 2-3 sentences with a call to action
- [ ] CTA button: Learn More (link to mettomanage.cloud)
- [ ] Expires: Set to 1 week from today

---

## Step 7: Monitor & Respond

- [ ] Check Google Business Profile weekly for new reviews

- [ ] Check insights: how many people viewed your profile, clicked your website, called you
